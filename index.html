🎵 07/03/2026

Wow - it's already July and it's very hot in New York. I might be one of the only ones, but I always kind of like a sweltering New York day. My coworker was playing The Necessaries the other day - what a group - members of the Modern Lovers, Red Crayola, and Arthur Russell. I heard one of their songs years ago on a radio show, but it seems like a whole anthology of theirs was released this year. I've also been listening to the synthy Touch Blue by Australian artist SCRAPS - pretty dance-y. I'm so happy @ brought out their new song - Autosmile. I've seen them perform this song a few times and I love it - it's so addictive and they're so talented. I was at Academy Records and noticed a Skip Spence (I love him) tribute album - I didn't buy it but have been listening to a Beck cover from the album quite a bit. I've also been enjoying new songs by This Is Lorelei and Westside Cowboy.

includes:
@
The Necessaries
SCRAPS

🎵 06/20/2026

I was traveling for a bit and was at a hardware store in Europe where they were playing Paul Simon's "The Boy in the Bubble." I know that song well because I really liked Patti Smith's cover when I was really young - I never really listened closely to the Paul Simon original, but it's really good. I've also been listening a lot to Danish Artist - Alvida Reiter Jakobsen. I tried going to the @, Allegra Krieger rooftop show in Brooklyn the other day but the line was too long to get in. Luckily I've seen @ a few times (they're so good live), but I would have liked to have seen Allegra Krieger. I included her song "Lingering" on here. I've been liking a new song by Polish band Coals - feat. Molina. I also always like everything Sacha Got (from La Femme) and Tentative do - they have a new cover out. Finally, I've been enjoying the band many shining windows.

includes:
Allegra Krieger
Alvida Reiter Jakobsen
many shining windows

🎵 06/05/2026

I'm one week late on this one - but I was traveling for a bit. I've been listening repeatedly to "Hide and Seek" by a band called The Eighteenth Day of May. They weren't around for that long and only released on album in 2005 - it's now being reissued. It's a bit folkier than what I feel like I usually listen to, but I really like it. I've loved Opal since I was a kid - I was listening to them the other day and have been listening to this song a lot. A bit of a different genre - but I've been listening to I.A.O.'s "The Clan" - off Warp Records and a band called Crescent, which is a side project of Movietone, who I also really love. Astrel K's - "Firma" - released a few years ago is really good - it really reminds me of Robert Wyatt (in the best way possible) Finally, I've really been liking Duval Timothy's new music. I was first introduced to him probably around 2019 or so by my friend. Really liking his new track called "loopy".

includes:
Duval Timothy
The Eighteenth Day Of May
Opal

🎵 05/15/2026

I can't believe it's already the middle of May. I haven't been listening to that much new music lately, so this one's a short one. I've included Aldous Harding on here a lot - her new album is finally out. I really like the song "Worms" on it. I've also been listening a lot to an artist named Eliana Glass. 2 Fridays ago, I saw Stephin Merritt from the Magnetic Fields perform a few songs at a bar in my neighborhood. Finally, last week I saw EXEK at Home Sweet Home - cool show, a lot of energy.

🎵 05/01/2026

I'm a day late with this one, but it's been a long week. I've been listening to AU band Tangled Shoelaces a ton - I first heard them some time last year - it's crazy that they were only kids when they made such beautiful music. The song on here is called "Oceans Away." Have also been listening a lot to another ocean related song by Beverley Martyn who sadly passed away this week. I've also - yet again - been listening a lot to Insecure Men (mostly their covers album). I was at a bar last week and saw one of the members - cool to see him in the neighborhood. I'm listening to Bauhaus/Tones On Tail adjacent band - Love and Rockets - great to get the mood up! Finally, I was in Tompkins and heard some guys playing ping pong blasting Blaze Foley on their speakers - unlikely speakers music. The guitars on this one remind me slightly of Donovan.

includes:
Tangled Shoelaces
Beverley Martyn
Insecure Men

🎵 04/16/2026

It's been a busy few weeks and I'm really tired, but there's always more music listening. I've been liking Wendy Eisenberg's "Meaning Business." It's really good and feels complex. I've also been listening to Aldous Harding & H. Hawkline's new track a lot - as always, another addictive track by Aldous Harding. I had never heard of French singer, Alain Chamfort, but have been listening to his song Chasseur d'Ivoire. I listened to the whole album, and found it a little too cheesy for me - but I really like this song. It's strange I had never heard of him because he's collaborated with Jacques Dutronc, Serge Gainsbourg, and Claude Francois. I also included a Etran de l'Air song I've been liking - I first heard about them last year, but never delved into their 2022 album. Strangely, I've also really been into this disco-y track by a band called Brooklyn Dreams - the hot New York weather has made it the perfect song for the season. Finally, I'm liking White Fence's new singles and included the newest one here.

includes:
White Fence
Etran de l'Air
Wendy Eisenberg

🎵 04/02/2026

On Wednesday, I went to a house show where an artist I really like from Detroit - named Dick Texas - performed. Her album from last year is so good. I've added the first track on here, but listen to the entire thing! I went to the york bar in the East Village and they were playing Tamaryn - I hadn't listened to her in years, but am now back on it. Last week, Konk was playing in the office - I had heard of them, but had never really listened. If you're on YouTube, watch their video for "Konk Party." It makes me want to be in Tompkins - in the summer - in the 1980s. Finally, I've been listening a lot to Gil Scott-Heron's "I'm New Here." It's a song from when he was older - it's really beautiful.

includes:
Gil Scott-Heron
Dick Texas
Konk

🎵 03/19/2026

I've been listening to a lot of Kristin Hersh, Throwing Muses, and Belly again. It reminds me of when I was young. I love Kristin Hersh and have been listening to her song "William's Cut" - I never really paid attention to it before. I've also really been liking a cover of Michael Hurley's "Wildegeeses" by Australian artist Field Commmander Ali - her whole new album is really good. I wish I had seen Michael Hurley at Union Pool before he passed away. I've been really obsessed with a song by Westerman called Blue Comanche - his voice is a bit different than stuff I would usually like, but the song is very addictive. Cameron Winter's new song - Warning - is great. So many people I like don't like him, but I do. St. Etienne's "Filthy" has been on my ears a lot - it's one of those songs like Groove Is in the Heart that makes you happy. Finally I heard a song by Goodtime John that I've been enjoying.

Kristin Hersh
Westerman
St. Etienne

🎵 03/05/2026

It's been a busy two weeks and I'm now feeling slightly sick. I've been listening to Aldous Harding's new song a lot - it's called One Stop. I've loved her last 3 albums and remember listening to Imagining My Man on repeat in 2017 on a freezing cold night in Montreal walking to the bus stop to NY. Her songs are so addictive. Randall Stephens' whole album - The Music I Love Is Gospel (Numero reissue) - is so good - perfect when you want to listen to soulful instrumental music. I hadn't paid attention to this song before, but really like it. Revisited Katy J Pearson listened to her song "Sky" a lot - love it. I was listening to a radio show and heard this smiles song - started singing along and was confused how I knew it. Realized that I liked a cover of this song by Pink Flames back in 2016 - it was somewhere far in my mind, but glad I rediscovered it. Finally, I went to a bar a few weeks ago where they were talking about and listening to Minnie Ripperton. They played a song that samples Rotary Connection by Nuyorican Soul - have been listening to this song a lot.

includes:
Nuyorican Soul
Katy J Pearson
Aldous Harding

🎵 02/26/2026

After a blizzard this past weekend, it felt fitting that I listened a lot to two artists from a cold part of the world. Listening to a lot of Molina & Jenny Hval from Scandinavia.
