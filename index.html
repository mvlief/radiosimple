🎵 03/19/2026

I've been listening to a lot of Kristin Hersh, Throwing Muses, and Belly again. It reminds me of when I was young. I love Kristin Hersh and have been listening to her song "William's Cut" - I never really paid attention to it before. I've also really been liking a cover of Michael Hurley's "Wildegeeses" by Australian artist Field Commmander Ali - her whole new album is really good. I wish I had seen Michael Hurley at Union Pool before he passed away. I've been really obsessed with a song by Westerman called Blue Comanche - his voice is a bit different than stuff I would usually like, but the song is very addictive. Cameron Winter's new song - Warning - is great. So many people I like don't like him, but I do. St. Etienne's "Filthy" has been on my ears a lot - it's one of those songs like Groove Is in the Heart that makes you happy. Finally I heard a song by Goodtime John that I've been enjoying.

includes:
Kristin Hersh
Westerman
St. Etienne

🎵 03/05/2026

It's been a busy two weeks and I'm now feeling slightly sick. I've been listening to Aldous Harding's new song a lot - it's called One Stop. I've loved her last 3 albums and remember listening to Imagining My Man on repeat in 2017 on a freezing cold night in Montreal walking to the bus stop to NY. Her songs are so addictive. Randall Stephens' whole album - The Music I Love Is Gospel (Numero reissue) - is so good - perfect when you want to listen to soulful instrumental music. I hadn't paid attention to this song before, but really like it. Revisited Katy J Pearson listened to her song "Sky" a lot - love it. I was listening to a radio show and heard this smiles song - started singing along and was confused how I knew it. Realized that I liked a cover of this song by Pink Flames back in 2016 - it was somewhere far in my mind, but glad I rediscovered it. Finally, I went to a bar a few weeks ago where they were talking about and listening to Minnie Ripperton. They played a song that samples Rotary Connection by Nuyorican Soul - have been listening to this song a lot.

includes:
Nuyorican Soul
Katy J Pearson
Aldous Harding

🎵 02/26/2026

After a blizzard this past weekend, it felt fitting that I listened a lot to two artists from a cold part of the world. Listening to a lot of Molina & Jenny Hval from Scandinavia. From the opposite side of the world (AU), I'm enjoying a new song by EXEK - he's coming to TV Eye soon. His song is named "Arrivederci Back Pain" - which I relate to haha; though I would like my back pain to arrivederci. Strangely, I had never before in my life listened to Ween - but I've been loving a song of their's called Pollo Asado. If you want to hear another cool song about tacos listen to Beck's "Satan Gave Me A Taco." Are there other good songs about tacos? Finally, I've been dancing to an 80s song by Chris & Cosey.

includes:
Jenny Hval
EXEK
Chris & Cosey

🎵 02/12/2026

Hi again. It's been two weeks since I last posted what I was listening to. I was out of the city the past two weekends, and listened to a lot while travelling. The Fire by The Lemon Twigs was on repeat on my long bus ride. I'm loving a song called Hey! Mr. Sky by Jackie O-Motherfucker - I think Weyes Blood sang some vocals for them when she was starting out. I've been revisiting Guillaume Teyssier this week, and I've been obsessively listening to a Jaye Bartell song, Swim Colleen (featuring Angel Olsen). I hadn't listened to Californian band, X, in a while - fun to listen to this song while walking in the morning.

includes:
Jaye Bartell
X
Jackie O-Motherfucker

🎵 01/29/2026

An old Hawaiian folk band - These Trails. CCFX, which I hadn't listened to since 2017 - but keep on re-hearing in NY cafes and restaurants.
